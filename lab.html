OZBEAT

Play music the way you normally do and OZBEAT fills the screen with it: cover art and artist photos as a soft moving background, lyrics synced line by line, and visuals that swell with the sound. Works with Spotify, the browser, any app in the Windows media controls and BluOS speakers. Cover on one screen, lyrics on the other, and it also runs as a screensaver. Written in Rust, nothing to install, free for personal use.
