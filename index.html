Common questions

How much does a website cost?

A one-page business card site starts at 9,900 CZK, a full business website at 24,900 CZK. After a short call you get a fixed price in writing. It doesn't change unless you change the brief.

How long will it take?

A typical website is live in 2 to 4 weeks. Most of that time depends on how quickly we get your texts and photos.

What do you need from me?

Half an hour on a call, a few sentences about your business, your logo and photos if you have them. We'll help with the rest, including the texts.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. We'll show you how to change texts and photos. If you'd rather not deal with it, we can look after the site for you every month.

What about the domain and hosting?

We'll sort it out. The domain is registered in your name, so the website always belongs to you, not to us.

Who am I actually dealing with?

OZLAB is a small software studio. You talk directly to the people building your site, with no middlemen. Contracts and invoices go through myNEST s.r.o., so everything is done properly.
